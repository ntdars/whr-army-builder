2026-09-09
Added a full Changelog page, linked from the Barracks footer.
Fixed Dwarf Miners (and any future unit priced "X pts for the first N models") not correctly charging for per-model options like Shields or Heavy Armour.
Fixed Bertrand the Brigand, Marksmen of Miragliano, Long Drong Slayer's Pirates, Birdmen of Catrazza, Ruglud's Armoured Orcs, Mengil Manhide's Company, Braganza's Besiegers, Vespero's Vendetta, Oglah Khan's Wolfboyz, and The Cursed Company showing "NaN pts" when added to a roster.
Collapsed the toolbar's secondary buttons (Bug report, Import/Export, Rename, Print/PDF) behind a "More actions" toggle on mobile.
Made Rule Flag warnings (composition, wargear, lore, house rules, etc.) individually collapsible on mobile.
Fixed a bug where Morathi (Dark Elves) couldn't take Arcane Items.
Fixed Chaos Warriors' and Beastmen's Battle Standard Bearers (Beastman/Minotaur/Centaur) not having access to Chaos Banners, matching Chaos Warband. Note: the "whole army must share one Chaos Power" part of that rule isn't cross-checked for these two armies, since neither has a theme system locking Mark selection the way Chaos Warband's does — track that part yourself.

2026-08-19
Fixed Bat Swarm, Rat Swarm, Spider Swarm, Scorpion Swarm, and Jungle Swarm to require a minimum of 3 models, matching the main-rulebook Swarms rule.
Fixed Carrion to require a minimum of 3 models, matching other Monstrous Flyers like Warhawk Riders.
Fixed a missing "Shield & Heavy Armour" option for Vampire Counts and Von Carstein Vampire Counts.
Added a Rule Flag for a Vampire Count/Lord holding a magic level while wearing armour or wielding a two-handed weapon.
Fixed Liberated Magic Items (Halflings) not being added to a unit's points cost or shown as a tag on the roster card.
Added a Rule Flag for Empire armies exceeding the auxiliary regiment limit.
Added the Heroic Bretonnia Army theme, with its own composition requirements.
